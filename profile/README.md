## SandrPod

Self-hosted execution infrastructure for AI agents — your clouds, your machines, your rules.

- **[sandrpod](https://github.com/sandrpod/sandrpod)** — the control plane. Go, Apache 2.0.
  Runs the unmodified E2B SDK against infrastructure you own.
- **[blog.sandrpod.com](https://blog.sandrpod.com)** — longer writeups, with the commands
  and the failures they were measured from.
